How the model works

Every number ties back to the app

ConnectClub is an ISO / super-agent channel: members are the closers, the company keeps an override on the residual portfolio they build. These are the marketed economics from CommissionsPage.js and the promotions in server.py, taken at the conservative end — with no paid channel, since there's no budget to fund one.

Unit economicsAssumption

Members earn ~25% of net residual plus a ~$150–$500 upfront proc commission per Credit-Card-Processing close (and ~15% + ~$50–$100 on Virtual-Assistant intros). The company retains a conservative $55/mo override per active merchant and $150 net per close, funding a lean bootstrap operation — no salaried acquisition team, since the product is already built and the field itself is the entire go-to-market motion.

What it means for the portfolio

Reading the model

The ask is small and bounded.

A bridge of roughly $46K, for about eight months, is the entire capital exposure. That is a rounding error against what a funded launch would cost, and it's a knowable, worst-case number — not an open-ended burn.

Participants get paid before the company does — on purpose.

Members earn commissions the moment a merchant closes, well before the company's own take clears its costs. That is what keeps the founding sales force selling and recruiting through the thin early months — the incentive has to work first for the flywheel to work at all.

The company's payback is the portfolio's payback.

Every dollar retained after month 15 is uncommitted cash the broader organization can redeploy — into the next vertical, into more field incentives, or straight back to the balance sheet. That's the "energy": a business line that funds its own growth and then funds what comes next.

This is a template, not a one-off.

Product built once, a lean bootstrap operation, and a commission-aligned field that recruits itself — that pattern is repeatable for the next vertical the portfolio decides to cobble together, with the recruiting-intensity table above as the tuning guide.

Method & caveats. A deterministic 60-month model built from the unit economics ConnectClub markets in-product, taken at the conservative end of the stated ranges, run from a $0 starting cash position with no paid-acquisition channel. Figures are undiscounted and assume the residual override and portfolio compound with only the modeled ~1.5%/mo runoff; they are a planning tool, not a forecast, and are sensitive to recruiting intensity, close rate, churn, and the override retained per merchant — all exposed as tunable assumptions. Member distributions are a parallel payout pool (the ISO's commission to the closer), not a company cash outflow — they are reported alongside company revenue, not subtracted from it. All approximate, per the app's own disclaimers. Generated from bootstrap_launch.py — edit the assumptions block and re-render.
