How the model works

Every number ties back to the app

ConnectClub is an ISO / super-agent channel: members are the closers, ConnectClub keeps an override on the residual portfolio they build. These are the marketed economics from CommissionsPage.js and the promotions in server.py, taken at the conservative end.

What it means

Reading the model

The referral is a growth engine, not a growth perk.

It compounds twice — more members feed more merchants onto a recurring portfolio that itself compounds. Losing it costs ~$25.8M over five years, far more than any line-item marketing budget it might replace.

Protect year one above all.

The burn trough lands around month 11 at roughly –$28K on a $1M seed. That is thin. A weaker referral loop, a higher CAC, or slower closes turns a rounding error into a raise — so year-one referral health is a survival metric, not a marketing KPI.

Instrument the loop before it fades.

The app already mints a referral_code, tracks invites, and rewards onboarded referrals in network credits. Watch active referrals ÷ active members monthly; if it drifts under ~0.05, the drop-off case — not the baseline — becomes the plan of record.

Residual runoff is the quiet tax.

Even intact, ~1.5%/mo of the merchant portfolio churns. New closes have to outrun that decay every month — which is exactly the job the referral-fed member base does. When referrals stall, runoff starts winning on the margin.
